Our Craftsmanship

Every gown carries the hours of the hands that made it.

Lara Wohti was founded on a simple belief — that Pakistani bridal wear deserves the same reverence for craft as any couture house in Paris or Milan. Every commission begins with hand-drawn embroidery patterns, moves through our karigars' needles thread by thread, and is finished with fabrics sourced for their weight, drape and longevity.

From the first sketch to the final fitting, nothing leaves our atelier until it is worthy of the day it was made for. We ship worldwide, and every bridal order includes a private virtual fitting for our clients abroad.
